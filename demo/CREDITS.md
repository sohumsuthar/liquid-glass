# demo/backdrop.avif · backdrop.webp

JWST image of the Lion Nebula (NIRCam and MIRI), obtained from NASA.
Credit: **NASA, ESA, CSA**.

NASA content is generally not subject to copyright in the United States and is
cleared for reuse, including on web pages, provided NASA is credited and the use
does not imply endorsement. The NASA insignia and logotypes are NOT covered by
that permission and are not used here.

Policy: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Resized to 1280px wide and encoded to AVIF/WebP; no other alteration. The demo
renders the credit on screen, which is what keeps the attribution requirement
satisfied.

This directory is not part of the npm package — `files` in package.json does not
include `demo`, so these images are GitHub-only and add nothing to install size.
